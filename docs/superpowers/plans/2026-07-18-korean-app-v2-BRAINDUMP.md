### Level 4 first half (review key for Task 6A's output)

- **4.01 -(으)면 -(으)ㄹ수록** — stem+(으)면 … stem+(으)ㄹ수록; the -(으)면 clause is
  optional/repeatable (보면 볼수록). Traps: ㄹ-stems attach directly (살수록); 하다 → 할수록.
  Pitfalls: (1) using past tense inside 수록; (2) forgetting 더 in the result clause when
  "more" is meant (갈수록 더 좋아요). Bridge: plain -(으)면 conditionals (TTMIK 2.23-ish).
  Note: 갈수록 alone = "increasingly (over time)" — worth a grammar.note.
- **4.03 -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다** — modifier ㄹ/을 + 리(dependent noun "reason/logic") + 가 없다;
  past: -았/었을 리가 없다. Pitfalls: (1) spacing — 갈 리가 없어요 (리 is a noun, space
  before it); (2) confusing with -(으)ㄹ 수 없다 (inability) — 리가 없다 = "it defies
  reason", about likelihood not ability. Bridge: -(으)ㄹ 수 없다 sentences (known, 12 hits)
  for the contrast; 것 같다 for "epistemic stance" feel.
- **4.04 -지요/-죠 (compressed)** — seek-agreement/confirmation ending; 죠 = contraction.
  One pitfall: rising vs falling intonation changes meaning (question vs soft assertion).
  Bridge: any known -아/어요 statements.
- **4.05 당신** — usage lesson, not conjugation. Content: Korean mostly AVOIDS "you";
  당신 is (a) spouses, (b) song/ad register, (c) confrontation ("당신 뭐야?"), (d) formal
  writing to unknown reader. Real strategies: name+씨, title (선생님), omission. Pitfalls:
  (1) translating English "you" reflexively with 당신 to strangers = rude; (2) 너 is fine
  only in 반말 between intimates. Vocab: address terms (저기요, 여러분, 씨, 님...) subject
  to leakage check. Bridge: sentences with omitted subjects or 씨.
- **4.06 Word Builder 動 (동)** — root "move". Candidate vocab (leakage filter decides):
  운동(known? likely), 운동장, 이동(하다), 활동, 동작, 자동, 자동차(known?), 동물(known?),
  행동, 감동(하다/받다), 동영상, 작동(하다), 부동산. Explain: hanja roots combine
  root+root; 하다 verbs derive. Known derivations go in explanation as "you already know".
- **4.09 -(으)면 안 되다** — conditional 면 + 안 되다 = prohibition ("if you do, it won't
  do"). Traps: 되다 spelling 돼요/됐어요; question form 하면 안 돼요? = asking permission
  (expect-yes). Pitfalls: (1) contrast with known -아/어도 되다 (permission) — the two are
  a system, teach as mirror; (2) double negative 안 하면 안 돼요 = "must do" (obligation
  idiom) — flag as note, it's common speech. Bridge: -아/어도 되다 sentences (known,
  status "known" 4.08) + plain -(으)면.
- **4.11 아무나/아무도/아무거나/아무것도** — 아무+person/thing + 나 (free choice,
  positive) vs 도 (NPI, requires negative verb). Pitfalls: (1) 아무도 with a positive verb
  (아무도 왔어요 ✗ → 아무도 안 왔어요); (2) 나-vs-도 meaning flip (아무거나 좋아요 "anything
  is fine" vs 아무것도 없어요 "there's nothing"). Bridge: negation patterns 안/못 (known)
  and 없다 sentences.
- **4.12 -아/어 보다** — attempt/experience: vowel-harmony 아/어 + 보다; imperative
  -아/어 보세요 = softened suggestion; past -아/어 봤어요 = "have tried/experienced".
  Traps: 보다 here is auxiliary, spaced per standard (가 보세요); vs -(으)ㄴ 적이 있다
  (known L2-ish? actually appears in curriculum known set? treat as familiar) for pure
  experience. Pitfalls: (1) 봐 보다 for 보다 itself (see: 봐 보세요 is colloquial but
  redundant — teach 한번 보세요); (2) confusing experience 봤어요 with plain "saw".
  Bridge: -아/어 주세요 sentences (known, 41 hits — same auxiliary construction shape).
- **4.13 Word Builder 不 (불/부)** — root "not". KEY RULE: 불 → 부 before ㄷ/ㅈ (부동산,
  부주의, 부정확) — this is the lesson's one real "grammar" fact. Candidates: 불가능(하다),
  불편(하다)(known?), 불안(하다), 불규칙(known — in TTMIK deck!), 불친절(하다), 부족(하다),
  불만, 불행(하다), 부자연스럽다. 불규칙 is known → explanation mention only.
- **4.15 아무 + noun + (이)나/도** — extends 4.11 to arbitrary nouns: 아무 때나 "anytime",
  아무 데도 "nowhere (with neg)", 아무 말도, 아무 문제도. Same 나/도 polarity rule.
  Pitfalls: (1) polarity again with full nouns; (2) 아무 데나/아무 데도 vs 어디나/어디도
  register nuance. Bridge: 4.11's own pattern is prerequisite — but it's same-file; use
  deck negation sentences + place-word sentences (에/에서, known).
- **4.16 Spacing part 1 (띄어쓰기)** — content lesson: (a) particles attach WITHOUT space
  (저는, 학교에서); (b) every other word spaced; (c) numbers+counters: space between number
  and counter (한 번, 두 명) but attached in arabic (2명); (d) 안/못 negation adverbs are
  separate words (안 먹어요). Pitfalls: (1) spacing particles (저 는 ✗); (2) 한번
  "once/just" (attached, adverbial idiom) vs 한 번 "one time (count)". Sentences: normal
  sentences; blankWord targets correctly-spaced chunks. No bridge requirement (0 ok — but
  2 deck sentences showing particle attachment are a nice touch).
- **4.17 Contractions part 1 (축약형)** — 것→거, 것이→게, 것은→건, 것을→걸, 나는→난,
  나를→날, 너는→넌, 무엇→뭐, 이것/그것/저것→이거/그거/저거, -지요→-죠. Pitfalls: (1)
  contractions are SPOKEN/casual-written register — avoid in formal writing; (2) 게 =
  것이 (subject) vs 게 in -게 되다 (different morpheme). Vocab: can be light (the lesson
  is pattern-focused); acceptable to have 4–6 fresh words used in example sentences.
- **4.18 가장/제일/최고** — superlative adverbs precede adj/adv: [군중] 중에서 제일 커요.
  가장 = neutral/written, 제일 = colloquial (user knows the word — 2 deck hits — but not
  as a taught point; full lesson justified), 최고 = NOUN "the best" (최고예요; 최고의 +
  noun). Pitfalls: (1) putting 제일 before a noun (제일 사람 ✗ → 제일 좋은 사람); (2)
  using 최고 as adverb (최고 빨라요 ✗ colloquialism to avoid). Bridge: 보다 comparison +
  중에서 sentences (known).
- **4.19 덜** — "less / not completely": 덜 + verb/adj, antonym of 더. Two senses: (a)
  comparative "less than" (이게 덜 비싸요), (b) incompleteness (덜 익었어요 undercooked,
  덜 말랐어요 not fully dry). Pitfalls: (1) reading 덜 only as "less" and missing the
  "not fully" sense; (2) 덜 with nouns (덜 돈 ✗). Bridge: 더/보다 sentences (known).
- **4.20 Sentence Building Drill #1** — NO new grammar. Structure ruling: summary =
  what drilling does; explanation lists the combinable points (4.01–4.19 "new" ones);
  vocab 0–4 allowed; 6–8 sentences EACH combining ≥2 Level-4 points taught so far
  (e.g. 볼수록 + 아/어지다 is not yet taught — only combine ALREADY-covered ids);
  blankWord = the chunk realizing the newest point. bridge: 2 deck sentences fine.
- **4.21 Spacing part 2** — dependent nouns take a space BEFORE them: 갈 수 있어요,
  할 것 같아요, 간 지 (time-since), 아는 것; auxiliary verbs after -아/어 usually spaced
  (가 보세요), compound single words attached (들어가다). Pitfalls: (1) -(으)ㄹ 거예요
  spacing (거 is dependent noun: 갈 거예요, not 갈거예요); (2) 수/지/것 jammed against
  the modifier. Bridge: known 수 있다 / 것 같다 deck sentences illustrate the spacing.
- **4.22 Word Builder 場 (장)** — root "place". Candidates: 시장(known?), 주차장,
  수영장, 운동장(if not used in 4.06 — coordinate!), 극장(known?), 공연장, 경기장,
  시험장, 현장, 장소(known?), 등장(하다), 입장(카드 both "entry" and "position" — pick
  one gloss). Batch-internal rule: 4.06/4.13/4.22 must not reuse each other's vocab.
- **4.23 Contractions part 2** — 되어→돼, 하여→해, 무엇을→뭘, 이것이→이게, 그러면→그럼,
  그런데→근데, 아이→애, 이야기→얘기, -아/어 주어→줘. Pitfalls: (1) 되/돼 spelling rule
  (되+어 = 돼 — test by substituting 하/해); (2) 근데 in formal writing. Same vocab
  latitude as 4.17.
- **4.24 훨씬** — "by far/much (more)": 훨씬 (+더/덜) + adj/adv; only comparative
  contexts. Pitfalls: (1) 훨씬 without any comparison target in mind (needs implicit
  than-what); (2) 많이 더 ✗ vs 훨씬 더 ✓ (많이 doesn't intensify comparatives).
  Bridge: 보다/더 sentences (known).
- **4.28 -아/어지다** — adjective → "become": vowel harmony + 지다, conjugates as a
  VERB (좋아져요/좋아졌어요/좋아질 거예요). Pitfalls: (1) attaching to verbs expecting
  "become" (that's -게 되다; verb+아/어지다 = passive, out of scope — one-line note);
  (2) present 좋아져요 = "keeps getting better" vs past 좋아졌어요 = "has become".
  Bridge: comparative sentences + 것 같다 (지는 것 같아요 is a natural combo).
- **4.29 -게 되다** — verb + 게 되다: "come to / end up / it was arranged": external
  circumstance, humble announcements (회사를 옮기게 됐어요). Pitfalls: (1) vs -아/어지다
  (adjectives become states; verbs come about via 게 되다); (2) 되다 spelling 돼요/됐어요.
  Bridge: -아/어서 or plain past sentences describing life events; 것 같다.
- **4.30 Sentence Building Drill #2** — same ruling as 4.20; combinable set now includes
  4.21–4.29 points.

### Level 5 (Tasks 6C/6D)

- **5.01 -(으)ㄹ 뻔했다** — modifier ㄹ + 뻔(dependent noun) + 했다; ALWAYS past
  (뻔해요 ✗ in this meaning). Means the thing did NOT happen. Pitfalls: (1) using it for
  something that partially happened (넘어질 뻔했어요 = did NOT fall); (2) spacing 갈
  뻔했어요. Idiom: 큰일 날 뻔했다. Bridge: past-tense narrative sentences; -아/어서 cause.
- **5.03 수고** — expression lesson: 수고하세요 (leaving someone still working),
  수고하셨습니다 (they finished; to superiors prefer 고생 많으셨습니다 — nuance note),
  수고했어(요) (to juniors/peers). Pitfalls: (1) 수고하세요 upward to a much higher
  senior can be cheeky — safer alternatives; (2) don't use to customers/teachers in
  formal service contexts. Vocab: related effort words (고생, 노력 is known...) — leakage
  decides; expressions as pos "expr".
- **5.04 -나 보다** — inference from evidence, VERB stems + 있다/없다: 비가 오나 봐요;
  past: -았/었나 보다. Speaker infers, doesn't know. Pitfalls: (1) using with adjectives
  (that's 5.05 -(으)ㄴ가 보다 — the pair split is THE core fact); (2) first person
  (자기 얘기에 나 보다 = detached self-observation only: 내가 피곤한가 봐 ok, but explain).
  Bridge: 것 같다 sentences (known) — contrast opinion (것 같다) vs evidence (나 보다).
- **5.05 -(으)ㄴ가 보다** — same inference for ADJECTIVES + 이다 (present): 바쁜가 봐요,
  학생인가 봐요; verbs-in-past join 5.04's -았나 보다. Pitfalls: (1) 있다/없다 side with
  verbs (있나 보다, not 있은가 ✗); (2) future/guess about intent → 5.23's -(으)려나 보다
  (forward pointer note). Bridge: descriptive adjective sentences from deck.
- **5.06 Word Builder 文 (문)** — root "writing/text": 문장, 문법(check leakage — likely
  taught), 문서, 문학, 논문, 문자 (text message sense!), 주문 ✗ (different hanja 注文 —
  do not include), 신문(known?), 문화(known?). Known ones → explanation.
- **5.07 -자마자** — bare stem + 자마자, NO tense before it (먹자마자, not 먹었자마자 ✗
  — pitfall 1); works past/future via the main clause. Pitfall 2: 자마자 vs -고 나서
  (sequence without immediacy). Bridge: -고 나서 / sequential 고 sentences (known).
- **5.08 -(으)려고 하다** — intention/imminent: consonant stem+으려고, vowel/ㄹ+려고
  (살려고). Also "was about to (but…)" with -(으)려고 했는데. Pitfalls: (1) colloquial
  -(으)ㄹ려고/할라고 are nonstandard spellings — recognize, don't write; (2) vs -(으)러
  (known, movement purpose only: 가다/오다) — 러 needs motion verb, 려고 is general.
  Bridge: -(으)러 가다 sentences (known, taught 3.x).
- **5.09 -다가** — mid-action switch: stem+다가 (하다가); -았/었다가 = completed, then
  reversed/next (갔다가 왔어요); often marks accidental outcomes (뛰다가 넘어졌어요).
  Pitfalls: (1) 하다가 vs 했다가 nuance (mid-action vs after-completion); (2) subject
  must be SAME in both clauses. Bridge: sequential -고 sentences; past narratives.
- **5.10 명사 + -(이)라고 (말하다)** — quoting/labeling nouns: consonant+이라고,
  vowel+라고; 이것을 한국어로 뭐라고 해요? Pitfalls: (1) using -다고 with nouns (다고 is
  for verbs/adjectives — forward pointer to 5.17); (2) dropping 이 after consonants.
  Bridge: 이에요/예요 identity sentences (known, L1).
- **5.11 Drill 3** — ruling as 4.20; combinable = 5.01–5.10 "new" points (+L4 freely).
- **5.12 -(이)라는** — "the X called Y": contraction of -(이)라고 하는 + noun; 서울이라는
  도시. Pitfalls: (1) expanding wrongly (라고 하는 fine, 라고는 ✗ different meaning);
  (2) with people prefer -(이)라는 사람 for "someone called". Bridge: 5.10 is the direct
  prerequisite (same file); deck bridge = identity 이에요 sentences.
- **5.14 -(으)니까** — reason (speaker-internal/shared) + discovery use (-(으)니까
  after verb = "upon doing, found"). KEY contrasts vs -아/어서 (known): 니까 CAN take
  imperatives/propositives (더우니까 창문 여세요 ✓ / 더워서 여세요 ✗); politeness idioms
  (죄송해서/감사해서 constructions stay with 아/어서: 와 주셔서 감사합니다). Pitfalls:
  (1) commands after 아/어서; (2) overusing 니까 where 아/어서 is neutral (니까 can sound
  self-justifying). Bridge: -아/어서 reason sentences from deck (they exist under L3.x).
- **5.15 -(이)라도** — "at least / even (second-best)": noun+(이)라도 (물이라도
  마셔요); with question words = "any-" (뭐라도, 누구라도). Pitfalls: (1) vs -도 plain
  inclusion (커피도 = also; 커피라도 = if nothing better); (2) 라도 on subjects of praise
  (best-case) sounds sarcastic. Bridge: -도 particle sentences (known L1-2).
- **5.16 Narrative -(ㄴ/는)다** — plain/diary form: action verbs 받침+는다 / vowel+ㄴ다
  (먹는다/간다); adjectives + 이다 stay 다 (좋다, 학생이다); past -았/었다 all; 있다→있다.
  Uses: writing, diaries, headlines, self-talk, quoting base. Pitfalls: (1) adjectives
  with 는다 (좋는다 ✗); (2) mistaking narrative for rude 반말 (register note: to a
  listener it IS 반말; on paper it's neutral). Bridge: any known 요-form sentences
  (transforms illustrate the mapping).
- **5.17 -(ㄴ/는)다고 (말하다)** — reported statements: 5.16's plain form + 고 (하다/
  말하다): 간다고 했어요, 좋다고 해요, 학생이라고 해요 (nouns → 5.10's 이라고!).
  Pitfalls: (1) verb/adjective split (재미있다고 vs 간다고 — 있다 sides with 다고 plain);
  (2) tense goes INSIDE the quote (갔다고 했어요 "said he went" vs 간다고 했어요 "said he
  goes/will"). Bridge: 5.16 prerequisite (same file); deck bridge: 고 싶다고? no — use
  plain 말하다/듣다 sentences (known vocab).
- **5.18 -(으)ㄴ/는지** — embedded "whether/if": verbs 는지, adjectives 받침+은지/
  vowel+ㄴ지, past -았는지, future -(으)ㄹ지; with 알다/모르다/궁금하다/물어보다.
  Pitfalls: (1) using -는데 where -는지 is needed (sound-alike); (2) question word +
  는지 needs no 냐 (어디 가는지 알아요, not 가느냐고 — that's L6+ territory).
  Bridge: 알다/모르다 sentences (known, ㄹ 줄 알다 taught in deck).
- **5.19 동사 + -(으)라고 하다** — reported commands: consonant+으라고, vowel+라고
  (가라고 했어요); GIVE: 주라고 (give to 3rd) vs 달라고 (give to the SPEAKER) — the
  classic. Pitfalls: (1) 달라고/주라고 swap; (2) negative commands: -지 말라고 (말다
  imperative). Bridge: known imperative -(으)세요 sentences + 주세요 sentences (41 hits).
- **5.20 Drill 4** — ruling as 4.20; combinable = 5.12–5.19 (+ earlier freely).
- **5.21 Contractions 3 — object marker** — 를→ㄹ attached to vowel-final words: 너를→널,
  이거를→이걸, 뭐를→뭘, 나를→날; casual speech only. Pitfalls: (1) consonant-final can't
  contract (책을→책ㄹ ✗); (2) written formal register keeps 를. Light vocab latitude.
- **5.22 Word Builder 食 (식)** — root "food/eat": 식사(하다)(leakage?), 외식(하다),
  간식, 과식(하다), 식품, 식습관, 식욕, 회식 (also ties 會 — nice cross-reference note),
  식당(known), 음식(known) → explanation. Coordinate with 5.13 (會): 회식 goes in ONE of
  them (prefer 5.22, mention in 5.13).
- **5.23 -(으)려나 보다** — "I guess (it's going to / they intend to)": 5.08's (으)려
  + 나 보다: 비가 오려나 봐요. Pitfalls: (1) parsing — it's 려(고 하)+나 보다, so verbs
  only; (2) vs -(으)ㄹ 건가 보다 (near-synonym; keep as note). Bridge: 5.04/5.05
  prerequisites (same file); deck bridge: 것 같아요 future guesses.
- **5.24 말고 / -지 말고** — "not X but Y": noun+말고 (커피 말고 차 주세요); verb+지
  말고 + command/suggestion (걱정하지 말고 쉬세요). From 말다. Pitfalls: (1) 말고 vs
  아니고/아니라 (말고 = choose the other; 아니라 = factual correction — canonical trap);
  (2) -지 말고 needs a following directive, can't end a sentence. Bridge: known -지
  마세요 sentences (L3 negative commands) + 주세요 requests.
- **5.25 -에 비해서 / -는 편이다** — two-part lesson: N에 비해서 "compared to N";
  V-는 편이다 / Adj-(으)ㄴ 편이다 "tends to be on the ~ side" (편 = dependent noun,
  softener). Pitfalls: (1) 편이다 without the modifier form (커요 편이에요 ✗ → 큰
  편이에요); (2) extreme/absolute adjectives with 편 (최고인 편 ✗ — hedging the
  unhedgeable). Bridge: 보다 comparison sentences (known).
- **5.26 대신에 / -는 대신에** — "instead of / in exchange for": N 대신에; V-는 대신에
  (both directions: substitute AND trade-off: 비싼 대신에 오래 가요). Pitfalls: (1)
  missing the trade-off sense; (2) tense — 대신에 takes present 는 even for past events.
  Bridge: known contrast sentences (그런데/하지만) or 말고 once taught (same level — use
  deck 그렇지만-style instead).
- **5.27 -잖아(요) (compressed)** — shared-knowledge reminder "you know / remember".
  One pitfall: with wrong tone it scolds ("I told you!"). Bridge: 알다/기억하다 known
  sentences. 3 sentences, no vocab, compressed:true.
- **5.28 -(으)ㄹ 수밖에 없다** — "no choice but": modifier ㄹ + 수 + 밖에 + 없다;
  밖에 is an NPI particle — MUST pair with 없다 here. Also adjective use: 예쁠 수밖에
  없어요 "it can't help but be pretty". Pitfalls: (1) 밖에 + positive verb (밖에 있어요
  = "is outside" — homograph trap!); (2) spacing: 수밖에 (밖에 attaches to 수).
  Bridge: known -(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다 sentences (12+24 hits — the direct ancestor).
- **5.29 -았/었다고, -(으)ㄹ 거라고** — reported past & future: past plain 았다 + 고;
  future = ㄹ 거 + (이)라고 → 갈 거라고 했어요 (거 is a NOUN, so it takes 이라고 — the
  aha-moment; explicitly connect 5.10). Pitfalls: (1) ㄹ 거다고 ✗; (2) 았다고 요-form
  quotes (았어요라고 ✗ — quotes use plain form). Bridge: 5.16/5.17 prerequisites; deck
  bridge: -(으)ㄹ 거예요 future sentences (known, 77 hits).
- **5.30 Drill 5** — ruling as 4.20; combinable = all Level 5 taught points.

## PART B — Behavioral rulings for Tasks 7–11 (edge cases the plan left open)

**Lesson player (Task 7)**
- Persist per-lesson `{step, done, best, srs}` in store.lessons(). `step` saves the
  furthest step index reached (resume support).
- `done` = quiz step submitted, ANY score. `best` = max score % across attempts.
- On FIRST completion only: seed `srs = schedule(null, 2, now)`. Re-completions never
  reset an existing srs state.
- Lesson unlock rule: lesson N is unlocked iff previous non-known lesson is done
  ("known"/no-body lessons are auto-skipped in the chain). First lesson always unlocked.
- Quiz generation: from the lesson's OWN vocab+sentences only: type-the-Korean
  (en→type ko) for 4 random vocab + 2 sentence translations; listen-and-type for 2
  items IF hasKorean() else substitute 2 more typed items. Compressed lessons: 3 items,
  sentences only. Wrong answers → weak pile with kind "quiz".
- Bridge panel: render each bridge ko + en looked up from TTMIK_SENTENCES by exact ko
  match + 🔊 (gated by hasKorean()). Lookup miss (shouldn't happen — validated) renders
  ko alone, never crashes.

**Lesson review (Task 7/11)**
- A lesson is "review due" when `srs.due <= end of today` (local).
- Review session = 4 random quiz items from that lesson (2 if compressed). Result maps
  to ONE grade for the whole lesson: 100% → 3 (Easy), ≥70% → 2 (Good), else 0 (Again).
  Apply via `schedule(cur, grade, now)`, save back. (One grade per lesson per session —
  don't grade per-question.)
- Multiple lessons due: Today surfaces the most overdue first; the rest queue after.

**Today flow (Task 11)**
- days[dayKey] = `{right, wrong, done: [], dict: [sentence indexes]}`. dayKey = local
  YYYY-MM-DD (reuse the cards module's dayKey).
- Leg completion, appended to done[]: "lesson" = completed a lesson OR a due review
  today; "cards" = card queue exhausted for today (counts().nw+learn+due === 0 at any
  point today) OR ≥20 grades today; "dict" = ≥1 completed round (all 5 graded).
- Ring closes when done[] holds all three. Streak = consecutive days with all three,
  counting back from today (or from yesterday when today isn't complete yet) — computed
  on the fly from days{}, never stored.
- If lesson content is exhausted (all done, none due): the lesson leg shows "All caught
  up" and counts as complete for the day.

**Dictation (Task 9)**
- Sentence identity = index in TTMIK_SENTENCES. A round = 5 sentences uniformly random
  from the selected level filter, excluding indexes present in any days[*].dict of the
  last 3 calendar days; if the filtered pool < 5, allow repeats (never block).
- Level filter from `lesson` prefix ("1."/"2."/"3."); "all" = any with non-empty ko.
- Grade with grader.grade(ko, typed); wrong → weak pile kind "dictation" (key = ko,
  en stored). Each item logs right/wrong to days. Replay ≤3 times + one slow replay
  (rate 0.7) — unlimited replays would make it a copying exercise.
- No Korean voice (hasKorean() false): dictation library shows a notice card and the
  drill is disabled (typing what you can't hear is nonsense) — the Today dict leg then
  auto-counts as complete so the streak isn't hostage to missing voices.

**Weak items (Tasks 7/9/10)**
- Key = ko string. Value `{en, kind, day}`. Retire (delete) on first correct answer in
  the weak drill. The drill: type-the-Korean from en, 10 max per round, oldest first.

**Cards (Task 8)**
- Port v1 mechanics unchanged (lag200, sibling bury, undo token, day-rollover meta);
  newPerDay lives in store.settings() (single source; cardsMeta keeps day/introduced).
- Words browser: list WORDSNEXT with search (ko/en substring, case-insensitive on en);
  tapping reveals gloss; show r rank; no SRS actions there.

**Me (Task 11)**
- Retention strip: last 30 days, per-day bar of right/(right+wrong); days with no
  events render as gaps (not 0%).
- Backup: download exportAll() as `kov2-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`; import = file input →
  confirm card ("replaces matching keys, keeps everything else") → importAll → reload.
